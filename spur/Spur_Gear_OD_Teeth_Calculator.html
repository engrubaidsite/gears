10. Assumptions

  • External involute spur gear, 20° full-depth teeth (addendum m, dedendum 1.25 m), no profile shift, standard rack cutter.
  • The module is found from OD: m = da ÷ (z + 2). A non-standard result is flagged: standard modules are 1, 1.25, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10 mm.
  • Backlash needs a centre distance, so the mating gear is assumed to have z₂ teeth (default: same as z). The calculated backlash is shared equally: each tooth is made thinner by jt ÷ 2 on the pitch circle, and the same angle is removed on every circle.
  • Below the base circle (root circle inside it, z less than about 42) there is no involute: αr and inv αr do not exist and the flank is a radial straight line, so ψr = ψb.
  • ISO/TR 10064-2 backlash formula as used in the earlier calculators; the standard itself was not opened.
